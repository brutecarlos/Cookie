Privacy Policy — Cookie extension

Last updated: 2026-08-15

Summary
This extension does not collect personal data. It is designed to run locally in your browser and to preserve your privacy.

What we store
- `installTime` (timestamp) and a locally-generated `seed` are stored in `chrome.storage.local` to deterministically select the daily quote. These values never leave the user's browser and are not transmitted to any server.
No analytics or counters

- The extension does not collect analytics or counters. It stores `installTime` and a local `seed` only to deterministically select the daily quote. These values never leave the user's browser and are not transmitted to any server.

Third-party services
None. The extension does not send data to external analytics, advertising, or storage services.

Data retention
Data stored in `chrome.storage.local` remains until the user removes the extension or explicitly clears browser storage. Reinstalling the extension generates a new `installTime` and `seed`.
